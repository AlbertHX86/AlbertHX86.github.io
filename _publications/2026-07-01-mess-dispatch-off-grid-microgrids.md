**Abstract.** Isolated electrical networks, such as those found on remote islands or in rural communities, heavily depend on off-grid microgrids (MGs) to meet their power demands. As these systems increasingly incorporate renewable generation, the inherent unpredictability of load consumption and energy production poses significant threats to system resilience and voltage stability. While traditional storage solutions lack adaptability, mobile energy storage systems (MESS) introduce dynamic spatial flexibility, offering a robust alternative for managing system contingencies. This paper proposes a coordinated spatial-temporal dispatch framework for MESS to enhance the resilience of autonomous MGs. An advanced hybrid deep learning architecture, combining a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) and a Bidirectional Gated Recurrent Unit (BiGRU), is employed to deliver highly accurate day-ahead predictions for solar output. To capture the probabilistic nature of these predictions, a Non-Parametric Kernel Density Estimation (NPKDE) technique is used. An operational scheduling model is then formulated for a modified off-grid IEEE 33-node test feeder, integrating radial power flow constraints, battery dynamics, and a transportation network modeled via the Floyd-Warshall algorithm. Numerical simulations demonstrate that the proposed routing and scheduling methodology helps reduce load reduction, minimize photovoltaic curtailment, and enhance MG resilience.
